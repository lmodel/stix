# Unified Cyber Ontology (UCO) 1.5.0

UCO is an ontology for the cyber domain, published by ucoProject. Its 1.5.0 release is the commit `7ebb3957e9e9a2e1bb9c66cd1ede8c912a726344`, which the term extractor pins.[^extract_uco_terms.py] The schema binds UCO's namespaces as `uco-core`, `uco-observable`, `uco-identity`, `uco-location`, `uco-marking` and `uco-tool`, and maps to UCO's terms where the meaning matches.[^schema-stix.yaml] [Scope, influences and mappings](../explanations/scope-influences-and-mappings.md) lists them.
