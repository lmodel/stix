# Datasets

* [stix LinkML schema](stix-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML schema of STIX 2.1: the umbrella `src/stix/schema/stix.yaml` (schema id `https://w3id.org/lmodel/stix`, version 2.1) and its five modules, with 84 classes, 321 slots, 35 enums and 6 types. Its `lokf adapt` copy `.lokf/stix.yaml`, rooted on `StixObject` and `StixBundle`, is the bundle's domain schema.
* [stix_vocabularies module](stix-vocabularies-module.md) - The module `src/stix/schema/stix_vocabularies.yaml`: STIX 2.1's open vocabularies and closed enumerations as 35 enums, such as `StixIndustrySector` (`industry-sector-ov`) and `StixAgreement` (`opinion-enum`), each naming its STIX vocabulary in an annotation.
* [stix_core module](stix-core-module.md) - The module `src/stix/schema/stix_core.yaml`: the identifier and timestamp types, the shared slots such as `id`, `type` and `created_by_ref`, the abstract bases `StixObject`, `StixCommonObject`, `StixDomainObject`, `StixRelationshipObject` and `StixCyberObservableObject`, identity, the meta objects, and the dictionaries for hashes and extensions.
* [stix_sco module](stix-sco-module.md) - The module `src/stix/schema/stix_sco.yaml`: the 18 STIX cyber-observable objects, from `StixArtifact` to `StixX509Certificate`, the 12 predefined extensions such as `StixNtfsExtension`, and the types they embed.
* [stix_sdo module](stix-sdo-module.md) - The module `src/stix/schema/stix_sdo.yaml`: the STIX domain objects other than identity, such as `StixAttackPattern`, `StixIndicator`, `StixMalware` and `StixThreatActor`, and the dictionary of observed data's deprecated `objects`.
* [stix_sro module](stix-sro-module.md) - The module `src/stix/schema/stix_sro.yaml`: the two STIX relationship objects, `StixRelationship` and `StixSighting`, and the slots that join other objects, such as `source_ref`, `target_ref` and `sighting_of_ref`.
* [Copy of OASIS's STIX 2.1 JSON schemas](oasis-json-schemas-copy.md) - The copy under `upstream/` of OASIS's STIX 2.1 JSON schemas, examples and licence, taken from oasis-open/cti-stix2-json-schemas at commit `9af1db4` and checked file by file against pinned git object hashes. Tests hold the schema to it.
* [Examples and counter-examples](test-data.md) - The example data under `tests/data/`: 63 valid examples, one or more per class a concept may name as its type, and 158 counter-examples, one per constraint, each opening with a `# Fails at:` line that `tests/test_data.py` asserts.
* [Generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - The files generated from the schema and committed: `project/` (JSON Schema, OWL, SHACL, JSON-LD context and the rest), the Python datamodel in `src/stix/datamodel/`, the merged schema in `docs/schema/stix.yaml`, and the adapt copy `.lokf/stix.yaml`.
* [UCO 1.5.0 term list](uco-term-list.md) - `tests/uco/uco-1.5.0-terms.txt`: the 1,176 class and property IRIs of the Unified Cyber Ontology (UCO) 1.5.0, which `scripts/extract_uco_terms.py` extracts from UCO's own files, checked by hash, and against which `tests/test_mappings.py` checks every mapping.
* [Neighbours' adapt copies](neighbour-adapt-copies.md) - The released adapt copies of nine lmodel schemas under `tests/neighbours/`, each checked against its release's checksum and attestation, against which `tests/test_schema_rules.py` holds `.lokf/stix.yaml` apart: no shared class, enum or type, and only the listed shared keys.
